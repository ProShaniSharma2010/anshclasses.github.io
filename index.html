About Institute

Ansh Coaching Classes, Rajgarh – Pratapgarh (U.P.)

A disciplined coaching centre for Class 1 to 12 with focused study, doubt support and modern digital resources.

Ansh Coaching Classes provides systematic coaching from Class 1 to 12. We focus on building strong concepts in Maths, Science, English and other subjects, so that students can write accurate answers in the exam and score higher marks.

Our classrooms are clean and student-friendly. We maintain a calm, disciplined environment where students can concentrate on their studies without distractions. Every student is encouraged to ask questions, clarify doubts and revise regularly.

  • Separate batches for junior, middle and senior classes
  • Hindi + English explanation for better understanding
  • Regular parent–teacher interaction about performance
